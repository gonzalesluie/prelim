About the Company

In the bridal industry, if you have to explain a photograph then it isn't good enough. More than the flowers and the cake, weddings are made up of moments. Moments of glee. Moments of nostalgia. Moments of transitioning from single to married. And what makes a moment?

Emotions. The last time a father gets to dance with his daughter. The first time a guy meets his future wife via the singles game. The moment the maid-of-honor breaks down.

Ryan Raquino and his team are master visual storytellers who have witnessed many love stories culminating at the altar. Every detail is captured, no memory is missed. This can be attributed to anticipating specific highlights of the big day. Ryan's favorite is the groom's reaction when he first takes a glimpse of his bride which has proven priceless every time.

Not all love stories, though, are made the same. Every shoot is as unique as the couples who Ryan gets to know. He believes that client-photographer chemistry is a must for a successful partnership. Ryan derives both inspiration and joy from his couples, many of which he counts as friends to this day. After meeting Ryan Raquino and the rest of Blufish Photography, you would not want anyone else clicking the shutter.
